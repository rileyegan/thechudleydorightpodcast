The Chudley Do Right Podcast — Mac setup
=======================================

1. Keep this whole folder together (do not pick out a single file).

2. Open Terminal (Spotlight: Terminal) and paste these three lines,
   then press Return. Change Downloads to Desktop if that is where
   you unzipped:

     cd ~/Downloads/ChudleyDoRight-MacSetup
     chmod +x mac-setup/install-mac.sh
     ./mac-setup/install-mac.sh

   Homebrew may ask for your Mac password. That is expected.
   Source Record and DaVinci Resolve still need a click on the
   pages the installer opens (Resolve wants a free Blackmagic account).

3. Headphones on. On show day, join as Bobby:

     https://vdo.ninja/?room=thechudleydorightpodcast&push=bobby&label=Bobby

If macOS says the installer is from an unidentified developer, either
right-click mac-setup/install.command → Open, or run:

     xattr -cr ~/Downloads/ChudleyDoRight-MacSetup

More detail: mac-setup/README.md
